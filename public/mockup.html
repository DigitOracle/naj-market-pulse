Why it moved

Context ledger COINCIDED, NEVER CAUSED
Every rise or dip gets checked down a ladder before a word is said on camera: data artifact → one-off mega-deal → mix change → season → policy → rates → world. Dated events overlay the trend; Najma says "coincided with" — never "because of."
W31 PEAK · 3,500 SALES
Checked: no single deal above AED 675m; volume spread across 3 areas. Coincided with: two Dubai South launch registrations. That's a launch spike, not a market re-rating — and saying so is the credibility play.
STANDING CONTEXT · AED PEG
The dirham is pegged to the dollar — US rate decisions are Dubai mortgage news. Every pulse carries the current rate backdrop as one line.
HANDLED WITH CARE · REGION
Regional tension has historically moved capital into Dubai as often as out — safe-haven flows. Conflict is context for moves in either direction, never a one-way explanation.
