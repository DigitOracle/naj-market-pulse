## 4 · Why there is no text in the generated shot

**Generative video cannot be trusted with letterforms**, and this asset appears on every film we publish. So the shot is
generated clean and **NAJMA** and **PROVENANCE FOR PROPERTY** are composited in the edit afterwards, where the type is
perfect and identical every time.

Use `assets/brand/najma_opener_plate_9x16.jpg` as the layout reference for where they sit — wordmark at about 74% of

## 5 · Two things that may not survive the first render

**The avatar reference is head-and-shoulders.** The lean needs her forearm and hand in frame, which means HeyGen has to
extend her below the chest. If it does that badly — wrong proportions, a strange arm, clothing that changes — then
**fall back to a tighter frame**: chest-up, leaning in from frame left with her shoulder and upper arm against the
sculpture's edge. The contact still reads and nothing has to be invented.

**Contact is the hard part of this shot.** An arm that floats a few centimetres off the edge, or sinks into it, is the
single thing that will make it look generated. Check it frame by frame. If three attempts cannot land it, take the
fallback: she stands beside the N, close, one shoulder turned toward it, no contact. **A confident stand beats a bad
lean.**

## 6 · ⚠️ The naming collision — decide before this is rendered

The opener says **"Welcome to Najma."** Every episode currently ends **"Welcome to Azimuth. Dubai, decoded — one tap at
a time. Complexity into clarity."**

A viewer would be welcomed to two different brands in the same fifty seconds. **My recommendation: drop the second
welcome** — the sign-off becomes *"Dubai, decoded — one tap at a time. Complexity into clarity."* One word out, no
collision, both lines keep their character.

This is bible §16 open decision 3, and it cannot stay open once both names are spoken aloud in one film.
