## 1 · NAJ (HeyGen), ~80 s

> A client messages me: "I need a studio in Business Bay, fifty to seventy thousand a year, near the metro." Here's how I
> answer that in under a minute. I open Azimuth and tell it what they want. To rent. A studio. Fifty to seventy thousand.
> Business Bay. Near a metro. And that's the brief. Eleven buildings fit, ranked by what studios there actually rent for,
> from the Ejari contracts, not asking prices. Peninsula Two: typical rent sixty-five thousand, and twenty-six new
> lettings in the last two months. Capital Bay and The Court, around fifty thousand. Every number comes from the
> register. If it isn't registered, it isn't on the screen. I pick the ten I like, and choose what to send. One tap, and
> I have a single comparison sheet with a map, ready for my client. And if they want to see where these are, here they
> are in Business Bay, the ten buildings in gold. From a WhatsApp message to a professional shortlist, in under a
> minute. Welcome to Azimuth. Dubai, decoded, one tap at a time. Complexity into clarity.
