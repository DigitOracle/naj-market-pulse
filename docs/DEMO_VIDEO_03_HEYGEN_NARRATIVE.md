> Dubai is a big place. Sixty-four thousand buildings, forty-one districts — when you move here, it
> feels overwhelming. Let me walk you through how Azimuth guides you. So the client asks: I've heard
> Emaar and Sobha are the good developers — side by side, two bedrooms? Two taps: two and a half
> thousand sales this year against a thousand; twenty-two thousand dirhams a square metre against
> twenty-six. Sobha's the pricier product, Emaar the bigger book. I'm also looking at London — why
> Dubai? Two million dirhams: a hotel room here, a parking space there. At the door, four percent
> against nineteen. Income tax, zero against forty-five. On the sale, zero against twenty-four. New
> York? Two point three times the price, fifty-two percent income tax, and a yearly tax just to hold
> it. Monaco? Five times the price per square foot — two million buys nine square metres. A parking
> space. So — Emaar. And here's the two-bedroom she asked about: Marina Cove, level one, the plan
> itself, from the developer's own file. Welcome to Azimuth. Complexity into clarity.
