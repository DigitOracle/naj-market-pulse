## The narrative — Naj alone, telling it (18 Sep, fourth version)

Kendall, 18 Sep: *"Naj is the only one on screen … Naj is saying a client came to me and said Dubai is such a
big place, and asked me to help her navigate it. And then Naj says, it is a big place … She's the only one
speaking."* One voice, Naj's; she reports what the client asked and answers it. Numbers spelled out, "AED"
written as "dirhams".

> A client came to me and said, Naj, Dubai is such a big place — help me navigate it. And it is a big
> place: sixty-four thousand buildings, forty-one districts. So I opened Azimuth. She'd heard Emaar and
> Sobha were the good developers, so I put them side by side, for a two-bedroom: two and a half thousand
> sales this year against a thousand; twenty-two thousand dirhams a square metre against twenty-six.
> Sobha's the pricier product, Emaar the bigger book. Then she said, I'm also looking at London — why
> Dubai? Two million dirhams buys a hotel room here, and a parking space there. At the door, four percent
> against nineteen — tap any line, and it shows the working, and the source. Income tax, zero against
> forty-five. On the sale, zero against twenty-four. She asked about New York: two point three times the
> price, fifty-two percent income tax, and a yearly tax just to hold it. And Monaco: five times the price
> per square foot — two million buys nine square metres. A parking space. So — Dubai. And Emaar: ten and a
> half thousand sales this year, fifty-three billion dirhams, four hundred and fifteen projects — every
> one right here. Welcome to Azimuth. Complexity into clarity.
