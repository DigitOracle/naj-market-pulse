## How to read this

- **Geometry is not knowledge.** Every footprint in a district becomes a modelled block; that is what the twin draws, not what it knows. The gap between the two rows at the top is the honest state of the data.

- **The live layer is the scarce one.** It arrives only through the developer group, only for developers who post, and only for the units they are selling that week. It is also the only layer with an asking price on it.

- **DLD and MEED answer different questions.** DLD says what has actually sold and for how much; MEED says what is being built, at what stage and contract value. A building can appear in one and not the other.

- Heights, storeys and footprints come from our own massing on open survey footprints. Areas the model reports are an envelope (footprint × storeys), an upper bound, never a floor-area schedule.
