Five ways in
Whatever the client opens with, one of these five is the door. Each one is the whole path — follow it and the next question is already on the screen.
01THEY NAME A BUILDING
“I’m looking at the Mac — in Business Bay.”
They already have a tower in mind. Go straight to it and let the building answer everything else.
  1. FIND — type the name, tap the building
  2. the building page — where it sits, the real view, the floor stack
  3. keep scrolling — price per sq ft, the unit mix, what is actually available
  4. MAP — the same building with its amenities around it
start in FIND →
02THEY NAME AN AREA
“I want something in Business Bay. What is it like?”
No building yet — a district. Show them the area whole, then drop into the towers inside it.
  1. MAP — tap the area on the map
  2. the area briefing — what trades there, price per sq ft, what is launching
  3. the amenities layer — schools, metro, malls, parks, beaches — the walk from the door
  4. tap any tower — and you are on the building page
start in MAP →
03THEY ASK WHO BUILT IT
“Binghatti — are they any good? Will they finish it?”
The trainer’s second and third question. Answer it from the register, not from the brochure.
  1. MORE › HOMES — the developers, ten to a screen
  2. tap the developer — how long licensed, what they are known for, the portfolio
  3. the delivery record — completed, under way, cancelled — and who holds the escrow
  4. tap any property — down to the units and the price
start in HOMES →
04THEY OPEN WITH PRICE
“What does it cost per square foot round there?”
The most common opening line, and the easiest to answer badly. Give them Dubai first, then narrow.
  1. PULSE — median price per sq ft and the median ticket, Dubai-wide
  2. where the market is trading — the ranked areas, live
  3. tap an area — its own price, against the Dubai median
  4. tap a tower — price per sq ft bedroom by bedroom
start in PULSE →
05THEY ASK WHO LIVES THERE
“Show me where the Egyptians are staying.”
Either they want to be near their people, or they want to know so they can choose elsewhere. Same map answers both.
  1. MORE › RESIDENTS — the residents map, all of Dubai
  2. pick the nationality — the communities shade in behind it
  3. the ranked list — where they reach the highest share
  4. tap a community — the whole mix, then the towers in it
open RESIDENTS →
